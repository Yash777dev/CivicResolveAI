CivicResolve AI is a production-grade, explainable, role-based municipal grievance
redressal platform engineered to modernize public service delivery. Traditional municipal
systems suffer from opaque first-in-first-out triage, duplicate field dispatches,
and citizen distrust. Existing research proposals that attempt to apply deep neural

priority scoring engine (0-100) where every single score is broken down into
transparent mathematical contributions (immediate hazards, essential service outages,
critical facility proximity, prolonged outage duration). Authorized field officers
retain decision authority via an officer-in-the-loop override mechanism backed by
mandatory written justification and immutable audit logging.


2. PROBLEM STATEMENT & OBJECTIVES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Problem Statement:
Urban local bodies and municipal corporations (e.g. Greater Chennai Corporation, BBMP)
receive thousands of citizen grievances daily across water supply, sanitation, road
potholes, electrical hazards, and drainage. Current administrative workflows face:
1. High Triage Overhead: Manual assessment of grievance urgency is slow and arbitrary.
2. Opaque Prioritization: Citizens cannot understand why their complaint is delayed
   while another is resolved.
3. Redundant Field Dispatches: Lack of duplicate detection leads to multiple field
   crews sent to the same burst pipe or pothole cluster.
4. Black-Box AI Risks: Deep learning urgency scores cannot provide legal accountability
   or transparent reasons to citizens or auditors.

Project Objectives:
1. Deterministic Explainable Scoring: Build a mathematical priority engine (0-100)
   with visible fired rules and point additions.
2. Officer-in-the-Loop Governance: Support manual overrides with mandatory written
   justification and preserved historical audit trails.
3. Strict Lifecycle Workflow: Enforce a validated state machine
   (SUBMITTED -> TRIAGED -> ASSIGNED -> IN_PROGRESS -> RESOLVED -> CLOSED) with SLA tracking.
4. Duplicate Detection: Detect similar complaints within category and temporal windows
   using TF-IDF cosine similarity without leaking citizen privacy.
5. Multilingual Accessibility: Bilingual support for English and Tamil (தமிழ்)
   preserving Unicode verbatim in database storage, indexing, and display.
6. Robust Defensive Security: Prevent Insecure Direct Object References (IDOR),
   neutralize CSV Formula Injection (CWE-1236), and safely sandbox evidence uploads.

7. EXPLAINABLE PRIORITY ENGINE: MATHEMATICAL RUBRIC & RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Philosophy:
In civic governance, decisions must be legally accountable, transparent, and reproducible.
An uninterpretable neural network urgency percentage cannot explain why a citizen's issue
was handled before another. The CivicResolve AI engine is 100% deterministic:

Mathematical Model:
For complaint C, raw priority score is:
   P_raw = BaseCategoryWeight + SUM(Rule_i_Weight for all fired Rule_i)
   P_clamped = MIN(100, MAX(0, P_raw))

Priority Tiers:
- 0 to 39  : LOW (72 to 96 Hours SLA)
- 40 to 69 : MEDIUM (24 to 48 Hours SLA)
- 70 to 100: HIGH (4 to 12 Hours SLA - Emergency Dispatches)

Configurable Rule Set & Weights:
1. IMMEDIATE_DANGER (+30):
   Fired when text contains emergency signals: "fire", "spark", "explosion", "collapse",
   "electrocution", "fatal", "hazard", "தீ", "விபத்து" or structured flag.
2. ESSENTIAL_SERVICE (+20):
   Fired when potable water supply is cut, electricity transformer down, or major sewer burst.
3. CRITICAL_FACILITY (+15):
   Fired when grievance impacts a government hospital, clinic, primary school, or railway hub.
4. REPEAT_NEIGHBORHOOD (+10):
   Fired when multiple complaints are registered in the same locality/ward within 7 days.
5. LONG_DURATION (+10):
   Fired when outage duration is reported as >= 3 days.
6. VULNERABLE_IMPACT (+5):
   Fired when voluntarily disclosed impact on senior citizens, infants, or persons with disabilities.
7. CATEGORY_BASELINE (+5 to +10):
   Inherent baseline score assigned from municipal taxonomy.

Safety & Non-Discrimination Guardrails:
- No Duplicate Point Stacking: A keyword appearing multiple times fires the rule only once.
- Tone & Literacy Neutrality: No penalty for grammar, spelling, colloquialisms, or lack of photos.
- Zero Demographical Inferences: Income, religion, caste, and identity are never inferred.
- Officer Override: Officers can adjust scores with mandatory written justification and immutable logs.


8. COMPLAINT WORKFLOW STATE MACHINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Permitted Transitions Graph:
- SUBMITTED          -> TRIAGED, ASSIGNED
- TRIAGED            -> ASSIGNED, NEEDS_INFORMATION, ESCALATED
- ASSIGNED           -> IN_PROGRESS, NEEDS_INFORMATION, ESCALATED
- IN_PROGRESS        -> RESOLVED, NEEDS_INFORMATION, ESCALATED
- NEEDS_INFORMATION  -> IN_PROGRESS, ASSIGNED, RESOLVED
- RESOLVED           -> CLOSED, REOPENED
- CLOSED             -> REOPENED
- REOPENED           -> TRIAGED, ASSIGNED, IN_PROGRESS
- ESCALATED          -> IN_PROGRESS, RESOLVED

Enforcement Rules:
1. Unauthorized transitions (e.g. SUBMITTED -> RESOLVED directly) raise ValidationError (400).
2. Moving to RESOLVED strictly requires a mandatory resolution_summary (min 10 characters).
3. Citizens can request REOPENED if the underlying defect was not properly addressed.
4. SLA breaches trigger automatic ESCALATED state and notify departmental supervisors.
5. All transitions record previous_status, new_status, actor_id, timestamp, public note,
   and internal note in complaint_status_history.


9. DUPLICATE DETECTION SERVICE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Algorithm & Methodology:
- Rolling Temporal Window: Examines grievances filed within a 30-day window in the same category.
- Vectorizer: Scikit-Learn TfidfVectorizer with sublinear term frequency scaling.
- Similarity Computation: Pairwise cosine similarity matrix.
- Threshold: Complaints exceeding cosine similarity of 0.65 are flagged as potential duplicates.
- Privacy Preservation: Duplicate relationships are shown to municipal officers to coordinate
  field maintenance. Another citizen's personal identification or phone number is NEVER leaked.
- No Auto-Merging: The system never auto-closes or suppresses potential duplicates; human officers
  verify and link relationships.

11. SECURITY & DEFENSIVE ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Insecure Direct Object Reference (IDOR) Defense:
   In app/policies/permissions.py, all complaint retrievals check citizen_id == current_user.id
   if the actor is a citizen. Cross-tenant access is rejected with 403 Forbidden.

2. CSV Formula Injection Defense (CWE-1236):
   In app/services/analytics_service.py, all exported CSV string fields starting with
   '=', '+', '-', or '@' are escaped with a leading single quote ('), neutralizing
   formula execution in Microsoft Excel and LibreOffice Calc.

3. Path Traversal & Safe Evidence Uploads (CWE-22 / CWE-434):
   - Werkzeug secure_filename strips directory traversal paths (../).
   - Magic bytes / MIME validation restricts files strictly to image/jpeg, image/png, image/webp.
   - Files are stored under random UUIDv4 names in a sandboxed storage/uploads folder.
   - SHA-256 cryptographic checksums are stored in the database for integrity validation.

4. Public Tracking Dual Verification:
   The public tracking route (/track) requires both the public reference code and the last
   4 digits of the complainant's phone number or email prefix, completely preventing
   reference enumeration attacks.

5. Authentication & Passwords:
   - Passwords hashed with PBKDF2-SHA256 with random salt.
   - Short-lived JWT access tokens (60 min) with declarative @role_required() route guards.

16. COMPREHENSIVE VIVA VOCE QUESTIONS & DEFENSE ANSWERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q1: Why use a deterministic rule engine instead of a deep neural network (BERT/GPT) for priority?
A1: In public administration and administrative law, grievance decisions must be transparent,
    auditable, and legally defensible. A black-box deep neural network cannot explain why
    a specific complaint received an urgency score of 87% or be audited by an oversight committee.
    Our deterministic rule engine guarantees identical scores for identical inputs, breaks down
    each point contribution visibly, and allows municipal administrators to adjust rule weights.

Q2: How does the system handle keyword manipulation / spamming?
A2: The engine uses set-based deduplication (seen_rules). If an emergency keyword like "fire" or
    "spark" appears 5 times in the narrative, the rule triggers only once (+30 points).
    Furthermore, priority scores serve as advisory recommendations to human officers who verify
    claims against photographic evidence and override unwarranted scores with mandatory logs.

Q3: How do you prevent Insecure Direct Object References (IDOR)?
A3: In backend/app/policies/permissions.py, every complaint retrieval and update asserts that if
    the user has role 'citizen', complaint.citizen_id MUST equal current_user.id. Tampering with
    integer IDs in the URL returns 403 Forbidden and logs the security violation.

Q4: What is CSV Formula Injection and how is it mitigated?
A4: CSV Formula Injection (CWE-1236) occurs when an attacker inputs a cell starting with =, +, -,
    or @ (e.g. =cmd|' /C calc'!A0). When exported to Excel, arbitrary commands can execute.
    In app/services/analytics_service.py, all exported string values are inspected; if they begin
    with formula triggers, an apostrophe (') is prepended, neutralizing spreadsheet execution.

Q5: What happens if the ML classifier model file is missing?
A5: The system implements graceful architectural degradation. If the joblib model artifact is
    absent, the category classification service falls back to the deterministic keyword taxonomy
    dictionary without crashing, clearly displaying "Rule-based category suggestion".

Q6: How is citizen privacy preserved during Duplicate Detection?
